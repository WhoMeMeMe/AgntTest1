## SCRIPT

Rice cooker-mu nggak punya timer. Nggak punya otak. Tapi dia tahu persis kapan nasinya matang.

Banyak yang kira ada sensor pintar di dalamnya. Padahal, cuma ada satu magnet dan satu per.

Selama masih ada air, panci nggak bisa lebih panas dari seratus derajat. Magnet itu terus menempel, menahan tuas tetap di bawah.

Begitu airnya habis, suhu langsung naik. Dan di atas seratus derajat, logam itu kehilangan daya tariknya.

Magnet lepas. Per mendorong. Ceklek.

Jadi bunyi itu bukan alarm. Itu tanda airnya sudah habis.

## BEATS

1. Hook, 0–5 s — "Rice cooker-mu nggak punya timer… kapan nasinya matang."
   Camera sees: close-up of a plain one-lever rice cooker; the lever snaps up on its own; slow push-in.
2. Myth, 5–10 s — "Banyak yang kira… satu magnet dan satu per."
   Camera sees: rapid dive through the base into a cutaway: one small magnet on a spring under the inner pot.
3. Dive, 10–18 s — "Selama masih ada air… tuas tetap di bawah."
   Camera sees: cutaway side view, water bubbling around rice; magnet clamped to the pot bottom; lever held down.
4. Twist, 18–25 s — "Begitu airnya habis… kehilangan daya tariknya."
   Camera sees: water level gone, pot bottom starts to glow warmer; extreme macro of the magnet contact loosening.
5. Payoff, 25–29 s — "Magnet lepas. Per mendorong. Ceklek."
   Camera sees: magnet drops, spring extends, lever flips up — same action as the hook, now explained.
6. Close, 29–33 s — "Jadi bunyi itu bukan alarm… airnya sudah habis."
   Camera sees: slow pull-out; lid opens on steaming cooked rice.

## FACT CHECK

- Supported: boiling water pins the pot near 100 °C; once the water is gone the temperature rises and a
  Curie-point magnetic switch releases, letting a spring open the switch.
- Wording kept deliberately loose ("logam itu") because sources disagree on which part loses its
  magnetism and on the exact trip temperature (figures range from just above 100 °C to about 104 °C).
- Scope: applies to classic one-lever cookers. Digital / fuzzy-logic models use electronic sensors —
  say so in the description.
