# Skill: thumbnail

Tensaikun: thumbnails are a whole topic of their own, and this part is more about the creator than about the model. If the title and thumbnail are not good, it is very hard for a video to get views. The film itself can be generated as planned; **the thumbnail is the one place an image model especially helps**. Let the model write title options, the description, and chapters from the finished script — after the picture is locked, not instead of a script.

**[ours]** Our covers follow the ZacD-formula Shorts pattern (vertical frame, clear subject, same title on YouTube and Instagram), not Tensaikun's 16:9 triptych with a giant product word, and not a copy of any existing thumbnail.

Treat this as a **separate task**. Do not generate covers in the same breath as shot start-frames unless Mas Hanief asks, and even then keep files and prompts separate.

---

## 1. Titles

Draft **three** options. He showed a picker with three titles; we keep that habit.

Pattern (ZacD DNA):

- `How [concrete mechanism] Works` + one emoji
- `Why [strange fact]` + one emoji
- `What If [impossible scale]?` / `Could [X]?` / `How To [X]` + one emoji

Rules:

- Title Case
- One promise, no long clickbait
- The same string is the YouTube Shorts title **and** the Instagram Reels caption
- On the Instagram sample in the DNA doc: no hashtag pile, no long caption paragraph
- Add `(fiction)` when it is not a fact
- Never use their exact titles, and never put `ZACK D. FILMS` or Tensaikun branding in the title

Recommend one option and say why in one sentence.

## 2. Description and chapters

From the approved script, draft:

- 1–3 sentence description (what the video shows, in plain English). No fake claims about views or income.
- Chapters only if the video is long enough to need them. A 30 s Short often does not. Do not invent 7 tutorial chapters on a Short (that was the shape of *his* 10-minute video).
- Optional: a one-line fact source if we used one.

## 3. Cover / thumbnail picture

Two valid methods **[ours]**:

1. **Pick a frame** from the finished Short — the clearest still of the object or action, already 9:16. On the ZacD Instagram grid, covers are usually a 3D vertical frame, often with a fragment of the white subtitle visible, not a huge poster title.
2. **Generate a dedicated cover** with an image model (Tensaikun mentioned writing a prompt and picking reference images, then generating the thumbnail there). Use an *original* composition. Do not paste their thumbnails as references.

Cover rules:

- 9:16, subject large, readable at phone size and with the sound off
- Prefer the hook object or the payoff mechanism, not a typographic mural
- You may allow a short subtitle-like fragment in the lower third if it is *our* caption style (`#F7F6F5`, few words). Or leave text off the image and let the platform title do the work
- No giant wordmarks, no lime starburst, no other channel's logo, no misleading before/after
- Do not reuse Tensaikun's "Claude" triptych layout

Prompt a dedicated cover with the same *look* prefix as the style skill (cyan cyclorama or the actual scene grade), then add: `thumbnail still, one iconic moment, no logos, no watermarks`.
